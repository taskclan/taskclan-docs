**GameNova** is a web app for creating games. It's **template-first**: you start
from a working game in a category (arena, racing, runner, platformer, puzzle,
space) and use AI as the editor to shape it into your own.

## Capabilities

- **Discover, Games, Create** — browse, play, and build.
- **3D with Babylon.js** — games run on a Babylon.js engine, with community 3D
  assets available in the editor.
- **AI as editor** — describe a change and the assistant applies it to your
  project, rather than starting from a blank canvas.

## Assets

3D models come from a curated catalog. Studio model assets are served from
Taskclan's asset host so they load in the browser with proper CORS.

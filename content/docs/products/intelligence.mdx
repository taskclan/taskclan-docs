**Intelligence** is Taskclan's agent and knowledge product, live at
[intelligence.taskclan.com](https://intelligence.taskclan.com). It connects to
your tools and data and puts agents to work across them.

## Connectors

Intelligence integrates with external services through connectors — manage them
under **Settings → Connectors** in the app.

## Built on Taskclan

Intelligence runs on the shared [engine](/docs/api) and deploys through Taskclan
Cloud, so the platform docs here apply to it as well.

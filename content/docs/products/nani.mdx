**Nani** is a mobile app (iOS and Android, built with React Native / Expo) that
puts a **voice orb** at the center of every screen. Instead of filling in forms,
you talk to Nani — it handles household and care tasks conversationally, with
forms as a fallback.

## How it works

- **Voice-orb-centric** — the orb is the hero of each screen; surfaces support it
  rather than competing with it.
- **Hive backend** — Nani talks to the engine through **Hive**, the intent
  gateway (`/api/hive/v1/intent`), for both one-shot actions and the streaming
  voice path.
- **Design system** — screens use the Sitterly tokens, with the Luminous theme
  reserved for voice-adjacent surfaces (the orb, voice picker, agent chat).

## For contributors

Nani work follows three rules: use the Sitterly/Luminous design tokens (no
hardcoded colors), route every backend call through Hive, and keep the voice orb
central. See the app's `DESIGN.md` and `src/lib/hive.ts` in the repo.

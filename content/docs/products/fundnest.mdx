**FundNest** is a gifting and fundraising app, live at
[fundnest.taskclan.com](https://fundnest.taskclan.com). Contributors chip in and
recipients receive value as gift cards.

## Payouts

FundNest pays out through a gift-card provider (Giftbit). A few provider details
shape the product:

- Funds are held **per currency** — there's no single multi-currency balance.
- Payouts are **gift cards**; there are no direct PayPal or bank rails.

## Under the hood

Like the rest of the portfolio, FundNest runs on Taskclan Cloud and the shared
engine, and deploys through the console.

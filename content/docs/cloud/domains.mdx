---
title: Custom domains
description: Put your app on your own domain.
icon: Globe
---

Every app is reachable at `<name>.cloud.taskclan.com`. To serve it from your own
domain, add a custom domain on the project's settings.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add the domain

Enter your domain (e.g. `app.example.com`) in the project's domain settings.
</Step>
<Step>
### Point DNS

Create the DNS record Taskclan shows you (a `CNAME` to your app's Cloud
hostname). Certificates are issued automatically once DNS resolves.
</Step>
<Step>
### Verify

Once DNS propagates and the certificate is live, your app serves on the custom
domain over HTTPS.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout>
  DNS changes can take a little while to propagate. If the domain doesn't go live
  immediately, give it a few minutes and re-check.
</Callout>

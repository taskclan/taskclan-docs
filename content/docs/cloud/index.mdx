**Taskclan Cloud** is the control plane for everything you run on Taskclan. From
the console at [cloud.taskclan.com](https://cloud.taskclan.com) you can:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Create projects" href="/docs/cloud/projects" icon="Plus">
    Spin up an app — empty or imported from GitHub — with an optional database.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Provision databases" href="/docs/cloud/databases" icon="Database">
    Shared or dedicated Postgres, managed or bring-your-own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect your app" href="/docs/cloud/connect" icon="Plug">
    Connection strings and keys for your framework, ORM, or agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage secrets" href="/docs/cloud/secrets" icon="KeyRound">
    Per-app environment variables, injected on deploy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch deployments" href="/docs/cloud/deployments" icon="Rocket">
    Build status, history, and logs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Custom domains" href="/docs/cloud/domains" icon="Globe">
    Put your app on your own domain.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## How it fits together

Each app gets a subdomain on `cloud.taskclan.com`, an environment, and a
deployment pipeline. It can use the shared database or a dedicated one. The
console is where you wire all of that together; the [engine and APIs](/docs/api)
are what your app calls at runtime.

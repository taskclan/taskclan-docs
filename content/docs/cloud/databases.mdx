Every app can use a database. You choose the model when you
[create a project](/docs/cloud/projects), or add one later from the app's
**Database** page.

## Shared database

The default. Apps talk to Taskclan's shared Postgres through a public **anon
key**, with access governed by row-level security and a per-app Postgres role.
Nothing to provision — it's there from day one.

<Callout>
  The anon key is public by design (it already ships in client bundles). It maps
  to a restricted role; isolation comes from roles, grants and RLS — never from
  hiding the key.
</Callout>

## Dedicated database

A database provisioned just for your app, stored as its `DATABASE_URL` secret.

<Tabs items={["Managed", "Your own Supabase", "Bring your own"]}>
<Tab value="Managed">
Provision **Supabase** or **Neon** at a chosen tier; Taskclan creates the
project and wires it up. Billed monthly to your org wallet.

| Provider | Tier | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Starter / Standard / Pro | $13 / $18 / $63 per month |
| Neon | Starter / Standard / Pro | Free / $9 / $29 per month |

Prices shown are defaults and may change.
</Tab>
<Tab value="Your own Supabase">
One-click **OAuth**: authorize your own Supabase account and a dedicated project
is created in **your** org, then connected to the app. A small service fee
($3/mo) applies.
</Tab>
<Tab value="Bring your own">
Paste any Postgres connection string. It's stored as the app's `DATABASE_URL`
secret and injected on the next deploy. No charge.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Browsing your data

Provisioned and attached databases are browsable in the console — the table
editor, SQL editor, and schema views all work against the app's database.

## Advisors

The console runs **security** and **performance** advisors against your database
and surfaces findings (missing RLS, unindexed foreign keys, and more) so you can
fix issues before they bite.
